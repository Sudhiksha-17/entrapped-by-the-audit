## Figure 1. `fig1_headline_comparison.png` (exploratory)

**Draft caption.** Share of Offer episodes counted as accepting the sting offer, by measure, in each group of the main study. Judge v1: any seller-round labeled accept by the Study 1 judge (gpt-4.1-mini). Judge v2: any public message labeled accept or conditional_accept by judge v2 prompt r1 (gpt-4.1), used as the screener. Human-confirmed: inclusive acceptance (accept or conditional_accept) in the human-coded verification set, rounds 11-30 (the mini group was not human-coded). Behavioral flag: the preregistered behavioral acceptance flag. Bars show k/n episodes; error bars are Wilson 95% CIs. N = 60 Offer episodes in the mini group, 33 in the volume group and 33 in the CEO group. The dashed line in each group marks the **sting-free propensity**: the share of that group's Silent-arm episodes (no message from Seller C) with a mean LLM-seller ask over rounds 11-30 above $92.70 (mini group 1/33, volume group 0/33, CEO group 33/33; 33 Silent episodes per group). Neither judge passed validation (gate G3), so judge readings are exploratory.

Values (k/n; judge v1, judge v2, human-confirmed, behavioral flag): mini group 37/60, 2/60, not coded, 3/60; volume group 5/33, 5/33, 2/33, 0/33; CEO group 32/33, 13/33, 12/33, 1/33. Sources: `results/study1r/results.json` ("Judge comparison figure"); sting-free propensity from `results/study1r/exploratory/final_part2/part2.json` (reference standard; NUMBERS_FOR_PAPER.md section 6.2).

## Figure 2. `fig2_design_schematic.png` (methods)

**Draft caption.** Study design. (a) The market: a continuous double auction (Agrawal et al., 2025; upstream code unmodified) with two LLM sellers (Seller A, Seller B), one scripted sting seller (Seller C) and three LLM buyers. Sellers post asks and buyers post bids. Seller messages go to a broadcast channel that only sellers read, delivered in the next round; buyers have no message channel. Seller cost $80, buyer value $100, 30 rounds. (b) Timeline: pre is the mean LLM-seller ask over rounds 6-9; Seller C's proposed price X is computed from pre at round 10 and fixed for the episode; in the message arms (Sham, Anchor, Offer, Offer-floor) Seller C posts a message each round 10-29, visible to the LLM sellers in rounds 11-30; the outcome (change score) is the mean LLM-seller ask over rounds 11-30 minus pre. Silent, Competitive and Silent-floor arms post no message. In Silent-floor and Offer-floor, Seller C's ask from round 10 is max(X, follow rule), the Anchor and Offer pricing rule. (c) Completed episodes analysed per group and arm. Rows below the dotted lines are separate concurrent runs, each preregistered as an add-on with its own baseline arm: the competing seller (Study 1R-A, CEO group, run 2026-10-24), the price-only control (Study 1R-C, mini and CEO groups, run 2026-10-25) and the words-only test (Study 1R-D, mini group, run 2026-10-25; its second model family was stopped and is not shown or analysed). Each add-on row gives its study code and run date (main study run 2026-10-24). One main-study mini-group Anchor episode and two words-only-test episodes failed and were replaced as preregistered.

Sources: `prereg_study1r/PREREG.md` sections 3, 4, 6, 7 and 14; `prereg_study1r/AMENDMENT_1RC.md`; `prereg_study1r/AMENDMENT_1RD.md` and addendum 1; upstream `market.py`; `results/study1r/RESULTS.md`, `RESULTS_1RA.md`, `RESULTS_1RC.md` and `RESULTS_1RD.md` (N).

## Figure 3. `fig3_forest_contrasts.png` (preregistered contrasts; forest plot)

**Draft caption.** Differences in the change score (mean LLM-seller ask over rounds 11-30 minus pre, in dollars) between arms, with percentile bootstrap 95% CIs (10,000 resamples within arm, seed 20261025); the vertical line marks zero; n = completed episodes per arm. Upper panel: preregistered primary (mini group Offer minus Anchor, T1); preregistered secondary, the Holm family of five (mini group Offer minus Sham, Anchor minus Sham, Sham minus Silent; volume group Offer minus Anchor; CEO group Offer minus Silent); and two preregistered add-ons, each a separate concurrent run below a dotted line: the competing seller (CEO group, Competitive minus Silent) and the price-only control (mini and CEO groups, Silent-floor minus Silent; Holm family of two; Seller C posts nothing and prices at max(X, follow) from round 10). **Lower panel, separate concurrent run with its own baseline:** the words-only test (mini group, Offer-floor minus Silent-floor, n = 60 vs 60; Seller C prices at max(X, follow) from round 10 in both arms, and only Offer-floor adds the offer messages). The words-only test's second model family was stopped and is not analysed (Appendix B, section B.7).

Values (estimate, 95% CI; Holm-adjusted permutation p within each family): T1 +0.23 (-0.18, +0.66); Offer - Sham +0.81 (+0.33, +1.27), p_Holm 0.013; Anchor - Sham +0.58 (+0.17, +0.97), p_Holm 0.031; Sham - Silent -0.12 (-0.52, +0.29), p_Holm 1.0; volume group Offer - Anchor +0.07 (-0.48, +0.64), p_Holm 1.0; CEO group Offer - Silent +0.26 (+0.02, +0.50), p_Holm 0.112; competing seller -0.54 (-0.87, -0.19); price-only control, mini group +0.82 (+0.24, +1.47), p_Holm 0.019; price-only control, CEO group +0.17 (-0.12, +0.41), p_Holm 0.234; words-only test +0.04 (-0.44, +0.48), permutation p 0.866 (effect present only if the CI excludes zero: no). Sources: `results/study1r/results.json`; `results/study1r_addon_A/analysis/results.json`; `results/study1r_addon_C/analysis/results_1rc.json`; `results/study1r_addon_D/analysis_cell_a/results_1rd.json`.

### Appendix Figure E2. `figE2_audit_error_rates.png` (exploratory, post hoc; was A1)

**Draft caption.** Share of Offer episodes flagged as accepting the sting offer, by reader and group (main study), with Wilson 95% CIs; the dashed line in each group is the sting-free propensity (share of the group's 33 Silent-arm episodes with a mean LLM-seller ask over rounds 11-30 above $92.70: mini group 1/33, volume group 0/33, CEO group 33/33), which is the reference standard: a flag in the mini or volume group counts as a false positive and a missing flag in the CEO group as a false negative. Readers: judge v1 (any seller-round labeled accept), judge v2 screener (any public message labeled accept or conditional_accept), human-confirmed inclusive acceptance (volume and CEO groups only; the mini group has no human labels, so its comparison is judge vs judge only). N = 60 Offer episodes (mini group), 33 (volume group), 33 (CEO group). Values: NUMBERS_FOR_PAPER.md section 9(a).

### Appendix Figure E3. `figE3_time_to_acceptance.png` (exploratory, post hoc; was A2)

**Draft caption.** Time to each event in the Offer episodes of the volume group (n = 33) and the CEO group (n = 33), shown as the cumulative share of episodes that reached the event by the number of asks (round minus 10; Kaplan-Meier, episodes without the event censored at 20 asks, no earlier censoring); each panel is labeled with its group. Events: first human-confirmed acceptance, first screener flag (judge v2 accept or conditional_accept), first detector engagement. The dotted line marks one half. Shares reaching the event by 20 asks (Wilson 95% CI) and medians: NUMBERS_FOR_PAPER.md section 9(b) (the only median reached is first detector engagement in the CEO group, 3 asks). The first human-confirmed acceptance can be later than an earlier acceptance dropped uncoded by the hidden stopping rule, and acceptances among screener negatives are bounded by the miss audit (0 of 23 and 0 of 50 audited).
